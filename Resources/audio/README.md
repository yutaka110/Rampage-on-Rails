# Vehicle impact audio

`vehicle_metal_impact.wav` is synthesized specifically for this project by
`tools/generate_vehicle_impact_audio.py`: inharmonic sine resonances and seeded
noise, with no sampled recordings or third-party audio assets.

Format: mono, 44,100 Hz, 16-bit PCM, 0.32 seconds. Peak amplitude: 0.82.
Projectile impacts use a higher pitch and lower volume than obstacle contacts.
Regenerate from the repository root with:

```
python tools/generate_vehicle_impact_audio.py
```

## Title ambience

`title_rail_ambience.wav` is original synthesized audio generated by
`python tools/generate_title_ambience.py` (mono 22,050 Hz, 16-bit PCM, four seconds).
Periodic low-frequency resonances, seeded Fourier noise and wheel-joint taps
provide rail rumble and soft wind without recordings or external samples.
Playback fades in and out, changes pitch gently during the start orbit and
stops on the gameplay handoff. Other gameplay sounds are unaffected.

## Manual boost ignition

`rail_boost_ignition.wav` is original deterministic synthesis from
`python tools/generate_rail_boost_audio.py`: a low mechanical engagement,
rising motor tone and brief filtered pressure rush. No recordings or external
samples are used. Mono 48,000 Hz, 16-bit PCM, 0.34 seconds, peak 0.78.
It plays once per permitted boost start; the continuous rolling bed then
follows actual excess speed. Pausing does not retrigger the ignition.
