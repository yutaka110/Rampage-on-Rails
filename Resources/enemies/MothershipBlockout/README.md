# Mothership blockout

Original geometry generated by `tools/generate_mothership_blockout.py`.
This is a silhouette / staging prototype, not the final textured ship asset.

- Hull: 162 units wide, 132 long, approximately 34 high.
- Entrance: starts 130 units behind the cart after the cave exit, overtakes horizontally in 3.2 seconds, then holds 155 units ahead.
- The flight keeps a constant world altitude, 76 units above the rail at the outdoor transition end.
- Launch sockets: local X = -43 / +43, Y = -9.5, Z = 0.
- Deployment waits for the flyby and a brief reveal hold, then takes 3.1 seconds per enemy, staggered by 0.22 seconds.
- The ship advances slowly over the route after its reveal. Queued launches stay attached to their socket until departure.
- Only newly spawned short-course enemies beyond the outdoor transition use carrier deployment. Existing cave enemies and READY actors are preserved.
- Deployment actors are untargetable and cannot fire until reaching their combat slot.
- The cave exit triggers a eight-second introduction even if a previous enemy group remains. A wide lead-in gives way to a closer 48-degree portrait aimed directly at the ship. It holds the settled ship for approximately two seconds before returning as drones descend.
- The host gates new enemy fire and grants temporary player/vehicle damage protection during this introduction. Travel, hover movement and player aiming continue.
- During the portrait, vehicle framing is smoothly relaxed and gameplay HUD fades to 10% so the ship owns the screen. Both return with the combat view.
- The camera never turns behind the cart or directly upward. Subsequent waves do not repeat the flyby.

Shared placement and flight constants live in `application/course/RailMothershipPresentation.h`.
There is no boss health, collision gate, or final ship texture in this prototype.


The extended route uses `MothershipReactor.obj` as an original octagonal rear
reactor. Its normalized radius matches the gameplay target sphere. The carrier
socket is local `(0,-11,-70)`; the render-only hull never receives fake hits.
All three meshes can be rebuilt with `tools/generate_mothership_blockout.py`.
